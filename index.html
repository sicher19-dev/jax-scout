The Read

Same city, different team. Three new problems, one proven plan.

Round one was the 3-walk night — 5 innings, 1 run, and only 3 strikeouts against the contact grinders. Since then you've run off two 10-K starts and Sunday's 16-of-21 two-strike clinic. But Seattle changed more than you did: Julio Rodríguez is back (batting cleanup), and the deadline bought Brendan Donovan and Taylor Ward. Crawford and Raley — a third of the round-one card — are on the IL.

The three problems, in order: Donovan is the lowest-whiff bat we've ever graded against your stuff (13%) with real damage — the Antonacci tape from Sunday is exactly what happens if he sees soft. Julio is a 59%-fast-swing on a flat path — no hole, so he inherits the Colson containment rule: never up, never behind. Raleigh is still the K-or-HR bat, but he taxed you 7 pitches for a walk last time — this time he gets two putaway looks, then the zone.

Everything else got easier: the bench is a wasteland (.187–.240), Naylor has a proven answer (cambio K + GIDP in round one), and the pitcher's park forgives the mistakes the Trop doesn't. Win the three problem at-bats with discipline and this lineup is round one's — without the walks.

SPEAK GRIFFIN · 30 SEC

Round two, and they're better: Julio's back and the deadline bought Donovan and Ward. But you're better too — two 10-K games and Sunday's zero-walk clinic since these guys saw you. The plan didn't change; the discipline around three at-bats did.

Donovan sees nothing soft. Not one changeup — Sunday's Antonacci at-bats are what happens (97, 99, 95 off the barrel). Velocity, ladder, ground ball, shake hands. Julio gets the Colson treatment: never up, never behind, and if the count goes bad, the walk beats the mistake — the yard behind you is huge and the bench behind him is a wasteland. Raleigh gets two putaway looks, then the zone — no 7-pitch walks this time.

And the round-one tax: the walks were the whole story last time — all three on full-count nibbles. Attack Arozarena early, trust the curve (it's your hottest pitch — 55% whiffs the last three), keep the sinker down to the fast swings, and this park forgives everything else.
